How it works

From alert to resolution
in under 60 seconds

1
Alert fires from any source
Prometheus, Grafana, n8n, or direct API. AlertMind receives the alert and creates a ticket automatically with severity classification.
2
AI retrieves matching runbooks
Vector search scans your entire knowledge base — past incidents, runbooks, KB articles — and surfaces the most relevant context.
3
Llama 3.3 reasons and responds
Your local LLM analyzes the context and generates a precise root cause, step-by-step resolution, and prevention measures.
4
Engineer acts, ticket auto-updates
Engineer follows the checklist. On resolution, the ticket is closed and re-ingested into the KB — so AlertMind learns.
